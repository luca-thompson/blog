# Certainty

Principle among the things you must come to terms with when transitioning from childhood to adulthood is the worlds complete lack of any and all certainty. Perhaps it is just the developing of ones abilities to understand nuance that results in this great abscence of absolutes. No matter the cause; in adult life you will find absolutely no black or white. Right or wrong, even simple yesses and nos become nigh-on foreign as you start noticing ever more questions being answered with the same, rather exhausted, thing. Even the simplest of questions are answered with it: "How long is a piece of string?". It Depends. Because It all Depends, and of course It Depends. Its a complex problem or it requires a nuanced perspective or the issue is multifaceted or whatever else. There are always some sort of circumstances that can extenuate or contextualise everything. It is a downright tragedy. It also represents an unsung blessing of childhood. There is of course the oft spoken to spryness and lack of responsiblity largely enjoyed in youth, however certainty is possibly a greater gift. The ability to sleep under the warm and cozy folds of the blanket of absolute surety in the things one knows is almost never discussed. It is for this that children can, and frequently do, sleep so soundly.
