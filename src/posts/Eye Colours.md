# Eye Colours

The two most common eye colours are brown and blue. They exist in perfect opposition and can be likened to Earth and Sky, Gaia and Ouranos.

There is both a calmness and restfulness to brown eyes, the lens through which they look is one of sympathy and understanding. They ground you, silently confirming that the earth beneath your feet is as solid as it feels. Observation is thus realised through empathy, placing themselves somewhere deep in your mind where they can see the way you do. Through this complete comprehension is achieved.

The Sky, naturally, operates in the inverse manner. Empty and away from all else the sky is nudifying, it lays all bare to see. Blue eyes owe colour and nature to this alike. Promising a certain objectivity and impartialness through some great piercing power they allow observers to see each individual cog and wheel as it turns. Pure analysis is their prerogative as they scrutinize their subject.
