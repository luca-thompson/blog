# Two Rooms

And, like that, a door is closed. Each room cut off from the other, nothing more than flickers of light sneaking through the keyhole and whispers worming their way under the door. It isn't locked, neither side has barred the other from opening the door at all. It may as well be locked though, for both sides know the other would not, and possibly could not, bring themselves to even try the latch for some time. It wasn't shut without cause after all. And anyway, could you imagine the self-loathing brought on by an attempt at turning the handle, if that attempt was met with the click of a bolt set by key?

At one time, neither room could even tell the door was there; everything flowed between them unfettered. There wasn't a taste, cry, song, jest, jumper or idea that found obstruction as it passed the threshold from one to the other. Each room found itself conforming and stretching with the influence of the other; carpets changing to floorboards, new wallpapers and strangely shaped furniture appearing. Each used the other as muse and cause for change. The rooms grew with eachother, adding bends and strange dividers, trying different layouts of their decorations. Things were torn down, replaced, re-torn down and put back up after a second thought. They helped eachother too, on cold nights if a heater, or the will to switch it on, was broken it took only a little time for the warmth of one to flow into the other. The guests of each rooms profited from this openess too; jokes found larger runways on which to land and sobs could be consoled with more sympathy.

Maybe with times passing, in a  far-far future, a tentative hand shall pluck up with courage and try the door. If the handle does turn, and no bolt has been set, the door might be opened again. It would be slow; the hinges would be quite rusty with disuse by then, perhaps so much so the door can't quite open all the way. One imagines a grand discussion full of wistful laughing and perhaps a melancholy tear here or there, for the rooms would have much to reminisce about. They might decide that, in hindsight and with whatever they have learned in the meantime, shutting the door quite so abruptly mightn't have been the best way to go about things.
