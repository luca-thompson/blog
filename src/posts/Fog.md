# Fog

**Draft**

The clouds were quite out of sorts this morning, oh yes, very cross indeed. You see normally, as im sure you all know, they take their place in the sky some few thousand meters up above our heads. However some mornings, such as this one, someone messes up. Clearly there is someone, charged with managing such things of course, that has made some kind of error. A dial tunred a few clicks too far, or perhaps a lever that really ought to have been pulled down has been left sticking up, maybe theres some button usually depressed to stop this sort of thing from happening. In any case the clouds were well below their mark, in fact this morning they were so far off their mark they werent floating on air at all, they were skimming on the ground! Naturally you can understand their frustration, its quite embarrassing for them, even if out of their control. ....
